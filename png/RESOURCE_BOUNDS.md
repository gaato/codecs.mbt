# Resource bounds

What the PNG decoder holds, and what keeps each structure from growing
without limit. The rule: **every structure that grows with the input is
listed here with its bound**. A new one needs a row before it ships.

**Every row of "Enforced limits" names a test that checks the bound**, in its
Tests column: a backticked file path, then the quoted names of that file's
tests. A test counts only if it drives the structure to its limit, or checks
the bound after every step over images that push it.
`scripts/bounds_check.mbtx`, run by `scripts/gates.mbtx`, enforces the
naming; whether a named test really reaches the bound is checked in review.

The decoder holds two rows and a zlib decoder, whatever the image's height:
rows are handed out as they are decoded, and an interlaced image comes out
pass by pass, so nothing holds the image. Chunk data passes through, into
IHDR's and PLTE's fixed fields, into the zlib decoder, or only into the CRC
of a chunk that is skipped, and fixed-length ancillary chunks into fields;
only a chunk the caller asks to have reported is held whole, until its CRC
has passed. Sizes count 1 byte for a `Byte`. The CRC-32's
tables, 9 KiB computed at start-up, are shared by every decoder and not
counted.

## Enforced limits

| Structure | Bound | On overflow | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rows (`src/decoder.mbt`, `prev`, `cur`) | Two of 1 + the bytes of a row of the image's full width (ceil(width × bits a pixel / 8), computed in 64 bits by `@rules.row_bytes`), the filter type byte first: at most `max_row_bytes` bytes of row each, 8,000,000 by default and at most 1,000,000,000. Allocated once, at the step after `Header`, before any image data; a pass of an interlaced image uses the start of them | `LimitExceeded({ resource: Memory, needed: Some(row bytes), limit: max_row_bytes })` at the step after `Header`, before anything is allocated | `src/bounds_wbtest.mbt`: "max_row_bytes: a longer row is refused after Header, before anything is allocated", "memory: the zlib decoder, the palette, and two rows from the step after Header"; `src/internal/rules/rules_test.mbt`: "row_bytes: packed rows, in 64 bits, against the limit" |
| zlib decoder (`src/decoder.mbt`, `inflater`) | One `@zlib.Decoder` for the decoder's life: 46,592 bytes (see `deflate/RESOURCE_BOUNDS.md`), reset with the decoder | Cannot overflow: one decoder | `src/bounds_wbtest.mbt`: "memory: the zlib decoder, the palette, and two rows from the step after Header" |
| Palette (`src/decoder.mbt`, `plte`) | 768 bytes, 256 entries of three, allocated when the decoder is made | A PLTE of more than 768 bytes, or of a length that is not a multiple of 3, is `Corrupt` in an indexed-color image and left out in others | `src/decode_wbtest.mbt`: "refusals: PLTE", "a truecolor image's PLTE of a wrong length is left out" |
| Units held back (`src/decoder.mbt`, `held`, `ihdr`) | 8 bytes: the signature, a chunk's length and type, its CRC, or an fdAT's sequence number, one at a time; IHDR's 13 bytes; and an fcTL that comes before acTL, 26 bytes, kept for IDAT. Every other input byte is used as it comes | Cannot overflow: an IHDR of another length is `Corrupt` | `src/decode_wbtest.mbt`: "the image data in IDAT chunks of every size, split anyhow", "refusals: the signature, IHDR and the chunks' frame" |
| Output slice (`Decoder::step`) | Whole rows, as many of one pass as the caller's slice holds; a slice shorter than `room_needed` takes nothing | `NeedMoreOutput` | `src/bounds_wbtest.mbt`: "output slices: whole rows, at most the slice, whatever it is given"; `src/decode_wbtest.mbt`: "the answers: Header, Frame, the rows of each pass, FrameEnd, Done" |
| Reported chunk's block (`src/metadata.mbt`, `Block::data`) | Only for the chunk types `report` names: the chunk's data, or for iCCP, zTXt and iTXt their prefix and the inflated field, held until the CRC has passed and the block is handed out, then dropped. At most `max_chunk_bytes` (1 MiB by default, at most 100,000,000): a chunk copied as it is takes its length, checked at its head before anything is allocated; an inflated one grows by doubling from 256 bytes, and the zlib decoder is given room only up to the limit. Chunks not asked for are only read through their CRC, whatever their length | `LimitExceeded({ resource: Memory, needed: Some(chunk length), limit: max_chunk_bytes })` at the chunk's head; `LimitExceeded({ resource: Memory, needed: None, limit: max_chunk_bytes })` once the inflated data would pass it | `src/ancillary_wbtest.mbt`: "report: max_chunk_bytes bounds a block, as it comes and as it inflates", "report: a block waits for room, and memory counts it and the second zlib decoder", "report: step_into hands out blocks too, and a block grows past 256 bytes as it inflates"; `src/fuzz_wbtest.mbt`: "corrupted images raise errors or decode, the same under every split, within bounds" |
| Second zlib decoder (`src/metadata.mbt`, `Block::inflater`) | 46,592 bytes, made with the decoder only when `report` names iCCP, zTXt or iTXt, and reset for each of their chunks. A second one because APNG allows text between the fdAT chunks of one frame, while the image data's zlib stream is open | Cannot overflow: one decoder for the decoder's life | `src/ancillary_wbtest.mbt`: "report: a block waits for room, and memory counts it and the second zlib decoder" |
| Fixed-length chunks (`src/ancillary.mbt`, `Fields`; `src/decoder.mbt`, `scratch`) | 256 bytes into which a fixed-length chunk is read before its CRC, the longest such chunk (tRNS of 256 entries); 256 bytes of tRNS's alpha, 4 of sBIT, and a few words for the others, allocated when the decoder is made. A fixed-length chunk of another length is left out without being held | Cannot overflow: a longer chunk is left out | `src/ancillary_wbtest.mbt`: "fixed-length chunks with values out of range, or the wrong length, are left out", "tRNS: a gray level, a color, the palette's alpha; the bits above the bit depth left out" |
| Decoder memory (`Decoder::memory_usage`) | The zlib decoder, the palette and the two rows above: 47,360 bytes before the step after `Header`, then 47,360 + 2 × (1 + row bytes), the same to the end of the image; 47,360 again after `reset`. With chunks to report, plus the block of the one being read, and 46,592 for the second zlib decoder when iCCP, zTXt or iTXt is reported | Cannot overflow: each part is bounded by its own row | `src/bounds_wbtest.mbt`: "memory stays what the formula says after every step, for every format and split", "memory: the zlib decoder, the palette, and two rows from the step after Header"; `src/fuzz_wbtest.mbt`: "corrupted images raise errors or decode, the same under every split, within bounds" |
| One-shot image (`decode`, `pixels`) | The image's rows, deinterlaced: height × ceil(width × bits a pixel / 8) bytes, computed in 64 bits from IHDR and checked before anything is allocated against `max_output`: 536,870,912 bytes (512 MiB) by default, at most 2^31 − 1, what an array holds; then the streaming decoder's memory, bounded by its rows | `LimitExceeded({ resource: Output, needed: Some(bytes), limit: max_output })` | `src/convert_wbtest.mbt`: "decode: only checked rows, max_output before anything is allocated, is_signature", "decode: 512 MiB of rows by default, checked at the header", "decode: an image larger than an array holds is refused at its header" |
| Converter's table (`src/convert.mbt`, `Converter::table`) | 1,024 bytes: RGBA for each of 256 indices, whatever the palette's length, so that every index reads inside it | Cannot overflow: fixed size | `src/convert_wbtest.mbt`: "palette: entries with tRNS's alpha, indices past the palette opaque black" |
